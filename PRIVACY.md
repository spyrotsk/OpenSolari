# Privacy Policy for Open Solari

**Last Updated:** July 22, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the **Open Solari** application ("the App") collects, uses, and protects your information, with specific reference to our integration with Google Assistant and voice features.

## 1. Google Assistant Integration (App Actions)
The App supports Google's **App Actions**, allowing you to use voice commands via Google Assistant to interact with the application's features.

**What you need to know about using Google Assistant:**
- When you speak a voice command directed at the App, your voice is recorded and processed directly by Google's servers. The App **does not record, listen to, or store** your audio.
- Google Assistant analyzes your command and sends only a text string or an "intent" to the App (for example: "Open the main screen").
- The App uses this "intent" solely to execute the requested action locally on your device.

The use of Google Assistant is subject to Google's [Terms of Service](https://policies.google.com/terms) and [Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
The App does not collect personal data or files from your device in the background without your consent. Parameters received via App Actions are used locally and instantly to navigate within the application and provide the requested service.

## 3. Third-Party Data Sharing
Data received from voice commands (intent texts) is not shared, sold, or transferred to third-party services. Execution happens strictly locally between system services (Google Assistant) and the application itself.

## 4. User Rights
Because voice processing is handled by Google, you can manage your voice activity history and associated data directly from your Google account (at myactivity.google.com).

## 5. Contact
If you have any doubts or questions regarding this Privacy Policy or how the App handles Google Assistant commands, you can contact the developer.
